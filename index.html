Part 2 · Explain

Class feedback & expectations

👔

Professionalism EXCELLENT

Everyone was professional. You came on time, you were dressed well, and you were polite. Keep doing this! It is the first thing an employer notices.
🗣️

Language NEEDS WORK

Some students had weak language. Common problems:
  • Verb forms: "I am study" → "I study"; "it give" → "it gives"
  • Past tense: "Last year I join" → "Last year I joined"
  • Prepositions: "good in" → "good at"
  • Articles: "a engineer" → "an engineer"; "I am shy person" → "I am a shy person"
Expectation: practise speaking every day, not only the day before the exam.
🔍

Depth of answers NEEDS WORK

Some answers were too short. "Yes, I like teamwork." is not enough. The examiner wants to know why and when.
Expectation: every answer has an Answer + Reason + Example (see below).
📋

Preparedness BIGGEST PROBLEM

Some students did not prepare, so they could not answer as expected. You had the questions, the apps and sample answers before the interview. A prepared answer comes from practice, not from luck on the day.

The formula: Answer + Reason + Example

A · Answer

Answer the question directly in one sentence.

"My biggest strength is teamwork."

R · Reason

Say why. Use because…

"…because I listen to others and share ideas."

E · Example

Give a real example from your life.

"For example, in my LSC group project…"

Shallow vs deep: same question

Question: What is your biggest strength?

❌ Shallow

"Teamwork. I am good in teamwork."

Short, no reason, no example, one language error.

✅ Deep

My biggest strength is teamwork. I am good at it because I listen to others and I am happy to share ideas. For example, in my LSC group project, two members disagreed, so I helped them agree on one plan, and we finished on time.

What does "prepared" mean?

A prepared student did these things before the interview:

  • 📄 Read all the interview questions
  • ✍️ Wrote notes or a short script for each question
  • 🗣️ Practised out loud, many times, with a friend or family member
  • ⏱️ Practised with a timer (3–5 minutes)
  • 🧠 Prepared 2–3 real examples from their own life
  • 📚 Checked new vocabulary and pronunciation

How many did you do? Count them and be honest with yourself.
